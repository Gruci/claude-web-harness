JetBrains Mono
--------------
Delivered viewer artifacts embed the JetBrains Mono variable font subsets served by
Google Fonts, so font selection does not depend on a network request or a locally
installed copy for the characters those subsets cover. Uncovered characters
(including CJK) use the system fallback stack. JetBrains Mono is maintained at
https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono and distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1; the complete license text is in assets/JetBrainsMono-OFL.txt and in the
font CSS carried by every standalone HTML export.
